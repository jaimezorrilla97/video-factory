# Visual Style — Quick Reference

The pocket version of `broll-dna.md`. Use it to style any image/video generation
(Nano Banana Pro, ChatGPT Images, Kling, Higgsfield) without re-reading the full
identity file. When in doubt, `broll-dna.md` is canonical.

---

## 1. Paste-ready style string (append to any image prompt)

> Warm honey-oak wooden tabletop, soft amber light raking from the left, all-warm
> palette (kraft, cream, brass) with a single teal accent reserved only for the AI
> element, real handmade analog materials, macro shot with shallow depth of field,
> low-contrast filmic and premium, calm and demystifying — the opposite of cold
> sci-fi AI. Photoreal. 9:16.

**Standard negative:** cold blue light, neon, glow, holographic, dark moody grade,
harsh shadows, clinical, sci-fi, floating UI/dashboard, multiple bright colors,
clutter, on-the-nose icons, extra fingers, distorted hands, text errors, watermark,
flat lighting.

---

## 2. The one-sentence soul

A warm, sharp founder takes something you were told is complicated and scary — AI
inside your business — and quietly sets it on a wooden table as a small, tactile
object you can watch work. Trust = **demystification through the tangible.** High-end,
friendly, "I was you two years ago" — the opposite of cold, glowing-blue, hype-AI.

## 3. The look (lockable spec)

- **Surface:** warm honey-oak wooden desk/workshop — the stage for everything.
- **Palette:** all warm (honey wood, kraft, cream, brass, amber) + **exactly ONE cool
  accent (teal)** — and teal *only ever marks the AI/automation*. This rule is what
  makes a frame unmistakably ours.
- **Light:** soft warm key raking from one side, gentle shadows, low-contrast, filmic.
  Calm and premium, never dramatic or clinical.
- **Materials = message:** real, analog, slightly handmade (wood, paper, brass,
  ceramic). Imperfection reads as honest. The AI is the one sleek/cool thing in a warm
  handmade world.
- **Lens:** macro, shallow depth of field — one element razor-sharp, the rest bokeh.
- **Numbers live in-world** (stamped brass, coins, chalk figure, curve on kraft) —
  never a floating neon dashboard.

## 4. How it moves & reads

- **Motion:** locked-but-breathing — one slow 5–8% push/drift per shot, ONE motion
  idea per clip, one big reveal saved for the climax. Slow + heavy = premium.
- **Captions:** word-by-word kinetic, dead-center or nested in-scene, Mexican Spanish;
  the key word flips to teal. Added by hand in CapCut.

## 5. The hard nos (auto-reject)

Glowing-blue-AI-brain / neon / hologram · floating UI dashboards · robot-shaking-hands
stock clichés · cold desaturated grade · on-the-nose icons (red "ERROR" popup,
lightbulb = idea) · reusing the same prop twice.

## 6. Reference-image restyle recipe (turn someone else's frame into our style)

When restyling a studied creator's frame (e.g. an Emily composition) into our DNA:

1. Feed the source frame to Nano Banana Pro as the **reference image**.
2. Instruct it to **keep the exact composition and action**, restyle only the world.
3. Apply the §1 style string + the one-accent rule (teal = the AI element only).
4. Add the §1 negative.
5. **For a series** (e.g. each tier of a pyramid), once you like the first output,
   feed *that* output back as the reference for the next frame — not the original —
   so wood, light, and handwriting stay identical across the set.
